Our Story

Born from a Vision to Make Quality Education Accessible

EduRise Coaching Centre was founded in 2016 by a group of passionate educators who believed that every student — regardless of background — deserves access to quality guidance. What started as a small tuition room with 15 students has grown into a thriving institute with 500+ alumni who've gone on to crack boards, competitive exams, and build successful careers.

Our founder, with 20+ years in education, saw the gap between school teaching and what students truly need — personalised attention, real-world application, and a teacher who believes in them. EduRise was built to bridge that gap.

Innovative Teaching Methods

We blend traditional pedagogy with modern tools to make learning engaging and effective.

Student-First Philosophy

Every decision we make — from batch size to fee structure — is centred on student welfare.

Results-Driven Approach

We track each student's progress carefully and adapt our teaching until the goal is achieved.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, affordable, personalised coaching that empowers every student to achieve their academic and professional goals through disciplined learning and expert mentorship.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted coaching destination in West Bengal, known for academic excellence, student transformation, and a teaching community that genuinely cares about every learner.

Our Values

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2016

EduRise is Founded

Started with 15 students in a small rented room with a mission to make quality coaching accessible to all.

2017

First Batch of 100 Students

Word of mouth grew our enrollment to 100 students. We expanded to our own premises with 3 classrooms.

2019

Computer Lab Inauguration

Launched a state-of-the-art computer lab with 20 systems and began offering Computer Basics and Programming courses.

2021

Online Classes During Pandemic

Seamlessly transitioned to online learning during COVID-19, ensuring zero disruption in education for our students.

2023

400+ Alumni Milestone

Celebrated 400+ successful alumni with a grand felicitation ceremony, honouring our toppers and achievers.

2025

500+ Students & Growing Strong

Today, EduRise stands proud with 500+ alumni, 10+ expert teachers, and a 95% success rate. The journey continues!
