How to Join

Simple 4-Step Admission Process

Getting admitted to EduRise is straightforward. We've made the process simple and transparent so you can focus on what matters — learning.

1
Fill the Enquiry Form

Complete the form on this page with your details. Our counsellor will call you within 24 hours to discuss your requirements.

2
Attend a Free Demo Class

Experience our teaching methodology firsthand. We invite you for a free demo session so you can evaluate the quality yourself.

3
Choose Your Course & Batch

Select the course and timing batch that fits your schedule. Our counsellor will help you pick the right programme.

4
Pay Fees & Start Learning!

Pay the transparent fee (no hidden charges) and you're enrolled! Collect your study materials and start your EduRise journey.

Things to Keep Ready

Student's name and date of birth
Active mobile number for WhatsApp
Current class / last exam appeared
Preferred course and batch timing
Fee payment (cash / UPI / online)

Scholarship Available!

Merit-based scholarships of up to 20% are available for outstanding students. Sibling enrollments get a flat 10% discount on the second child's fees. Ask our counsellor for details.
