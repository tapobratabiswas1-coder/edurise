Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course at EduRise?

Simply visit our Admission page, fill in the enquiry form, and our team will contact you within 24 hours to confirm your seat. You can also walk into our centre directly or call/WhatsApp us for instant assistance.

What is the batch size at EduRise?

We maintain strict batch limits — usually 15–30 students depending on the course — to ensure every student gets individual attention from the teacher. This is one of our most appreciated features.

Do you offer trial classes before enrollment?

Yes! We offer one free demo class for all courses. This lets you experience our teaching style and methodology before making any financial commitment. Contact us to schedule yours.

Are there any scholarships or discounts available?

We offer merit-based scholarships for exceptionally bright students and sibling discounts. Students who performed in the top 10% of our previous batch receive a 20% fee concession on their next course.

What courses are available for working professionals?

We have specially designed weekend and evening batches for working professionals. The Spoken English, Computer Basics, Basic Programming, and Competitive Exam courses are all available in flexible timing slots to suit your schedule.
