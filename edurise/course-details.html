Course Overview

The EduRise Spoken English Programme is a comprehensive 3-month course designed to take students from basic English awareness to confident spoken fluency. Whether you're a student preparing for a job interview, a working professional aiming for a promotion, or someone who simply wants to express themselves confidently — this course is crafted for you.

Our methodology focuses on daily speaking practice, vocabulary building, grammar correction in context, and real-life conversation simulations. Classes are conducted in small batches ensuring everyone gets to speak every single day.

Who Should Join?

School & College Students

Build communication skills for presentations, interviews and career.

Working Professionals

Enhance spoken English for corporate communication and growth.

Job Seekers

Crack English-medium interviews and BPO/KPO selections.

Homemakers

Gain confidence to communicate in English in social settings.

What You'll Learn

Correct sentence construction and natural grammar usage in daily conversations

3,000+ vocabulary words with pronunciation, meaning, and usage in context

Public speaking and group discussion skills with confidence-building exercises

Interview preparation — mock HR rounds, GD practice, and presentation skills

Listening comprehension and accent neutralisation techniques

Email writing, formal communication, and business English basics

Daily conversation scripts — shopping, banking, workplace and social situations
